# Candidate A — Drag initiation threshold cluster

Add press-delay (150-200ms) or minimum distance threshold (5-8px) to prevent accidental drags on click/scroll.

## Pros

- Directly addresses "initiation threshold" gap (primary friction per UX matrix)
- Low risk, well-scoped behavioral change
- WCAG 2.5.1 (pointer gestures) alignment

## Cons

- May not address drop targeting or visual feedback
- Single-parameter fix could feel incomplete
