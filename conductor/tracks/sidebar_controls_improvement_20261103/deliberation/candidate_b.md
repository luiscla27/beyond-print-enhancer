# Candidate B — Logical grouping cluster

Visually group related controls (Layout, Print, Appearance, Advanced) with clear section headers, dividers, or cards; consistent ordering.

## Pros

- Directly addresses grouping/discoverability gaps
- Reduces cognitive load through structure
- No behavioral changes (purely visual)

## Cons

- Doesn't reduce density (may increase it with headers)
- Less impact on "scanability" than progressive disclosure
